Common Questions

What's the difference between the tiers?+

The 3-Month is the core Killshot Program — the full trading system, weekly coaching, daily signals, and scanner access. The 6-Month extends that with additional products, deeper mentorship, and full ecosystem access. The 12-Month is the complete transformation — a full year with Jay, fast-tracked LP pathway, and priority access to everything Jay builds going forward.

Is this a recurring subscription?+

No. All three options are one-time payments. No monthly billing, no hidden renewals. You pay once, you're in for the full duration.

What happens after my program ends?+

You step into Jay's broader ecosystem — signal groups, live sessions, and the education platform. For those who qualify, there's a path toward the LP structure and the regulated fund. The relationship doesn't end when the program does.

Can I upgrade mid-program?+

Yes — speak to Jay directly on your strategy call or via the community and he can arrange an upgrade. The cost difference between tiers is adjusted at the time of upgrade.

What platform is used for delivery?+

Everything is delivered through WHOP — your course content, community access, signals, and resources are all in one place once you're enrolled.
