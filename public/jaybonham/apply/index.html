FAQ

What is the Killshot system?+

The Killshot is Jay's proprietary trading strategy built and refined over years of active trading and hedge fund management. It includes specific entry rules, risk parameters, and backtesting frameworks. The system also includes an advanced variant called Killshot Gold, introduced in weeks 10–11.

Who is this program for?+

Traders at any level who are serious about developing a consistent, rules-based approach to the markets. You don't need to be a professional — but you do need to be ready to follow a structured system, do the mindset work, and commit to 12 weeks of real effort.

How does the coaching work?+

Jay checks in with you personally throughout the 12 weeks — weekly reviews, daily or semi-daily trade signals, and mindset accountability. This isn't a course you buy and forget. Jay monitors your progress and adjusts the coaching based on where you are.

What happens after the 12 weeks?+

You step into Jay's full ecosystem — proprietary scanners, signal groups, live trading sessions, and education platform. For those who qualify, there's a path into the broader company: LP (Liquidity Provider) structure and the regulated fund once it launches.

How many spots are available?+

The program is deliberately small. Jay works directly with each person, which means availability is limited. The strategy call will confirm whether there's a spot open and whether you're the right fit for the current cohort.

What's the strategy call?+

A 20-minute call with Jay to understand where you are as a trader, what you're trying to achieve, and whether the Killshot Program is the right next step for you. There's no hard pitch — if it's not the right fit, Jay will tell you that directly.
