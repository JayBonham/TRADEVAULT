09Questions

Questions, answered.

Is this a signals service?
No. The Trade Vault is an education platform and active trading community. Members get access to signals and copy trading systems, but the goal is for you to understand every trade, not just follow one.
What markets does it cover?
The main focus is US30 (Dow Jones), Nasdaq (NQ/NAS100) and Gold (XAU/USD). Andrew's Spectrum scanner also covers the Emini ES (S&P 500 futures).
What platform do I need?
MetaTrader 5 (MT5) and TradingView. Both are covered in the curriculum. You'll also need a broker, and Module 2 covers how to choose one.
Which educator should I choose on the Community plan?
It depends on the market. Jay and Adam focus on US30 with Killshot. Tyler trades Gold and NQ with Smartshot. Andrew covers US30, ES and Nasdaq with Spectrum. Completely new? Start with Jay: Killshot is the most established methodology in the Vault.
Can I upgrade later?
Yes. Start on Tools or Community and move up to Elite at any time from inside your member area.
Will I make money?
We won't promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Trading involves real risk. The Trade Vault gives you a proven methodology, real tools and educators who trade the same markets alongside you. The rest is up to you.
