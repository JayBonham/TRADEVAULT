FAQ

Questions.

Is this a signals service?
No. The Trade Vault is an education platform and active trading community. Elite members get access to signals and copy trading systems — but the goal is for you to understand every trade, not just follow one.
What markets does it cover?
The primary focus is US30 (Dow Jones), Nasdaq (NQ/NAS100), and Gold (XAU/USD). Andrew's Spectrum scanner also covers the Emini ES (S&P 500 futures).
What platform do I need?
MetaTrader 5 (MT5) and TradingView. Both are covered in the curriculum. You'll also need a broker — Module 2 covers how to choose one.
Which educator should I choose on the Starter plan?
Depends on the market. Jay and Adam focus on US30 with Killshot. Tyler trades Gold and NQ with Smartshot. Andrew covers US30, ES and Nasdaq with Spectrum. Completely new? Start with Jay — Killshot is the most established methodology in the Vault.
Can I upgrade from Starter to Elite?
Yes. You can upgrade at any time from inside your member area. The startup fee difference applies on upgrade.
Will I make money?
We won't promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Trading involves real risk. What The Trade Vault gives you is a proven methodology, real tools, and educators who trade the same markets alongside you. The rest is up to you.
