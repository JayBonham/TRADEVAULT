Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information The Trade Vault collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. We keep it to what we need to run your membership, and we never sell your data.

01Who we are

The Trade Vault ("we", "us") runs thetradevault.co and the Trade Vault membership. We're responsible for the personal information described here. Contact us about privacy at support@thetradevault.co.

02What we collect

WhenWhat

You use the siteStandard technical information our hosting provider records, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, kept in short-lived logs for security and troubleshooting.

03How we use it

  • To provide your membership: confirm your purchase, give you access to courses, add your TradingView username to the scanners, and approve you into the Telegram groups.
  • To talk to you about your membership: setup reminders, access confirmations, important changes and support replies.
  • To run the affiliate programme: credit referred sales, calculate commission and send payouts.
  • To keep things secure: prevent fraud, account sharing and abuse, and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Marketing: occasional emails about The Trade Vault. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

If you're in the UK or the European Economic Area, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you (your membership or affiliate agreement), our legitimate interests (running and protecting the business, and telling customers about similar services), legal obligations (such as keeping financial records), and your consent where we ask for it.

04Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the services we use to run The Trade Vault, and only as much as they need:

  • Whop: checkout, payments and memberships. Your setup choices (plan, scanners, educator and TradingView username) are also stored on your Whop membership so our trading tools can grant the right access.
  • GoHighLevel: our customer database and email platform.
  • Vercel and our database provider: hosting the website and storing membership and affiliate records.
  • TradingView: we give it your username to grant scanner access.
  • Telegram: for the community groups you join.
  • Our educators and team: those who manage access and support.
  • PayPal or a crypto network: to send affiliate payouts.

Affiliates never see who you are. If you join through an affiliate's link, they see only a masked version of your email (like jo***@gmail.com) and the payments that earned them commission.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our members or the public. We do not sell your personal information.

05International transfers

Some of the services above are based outside your country, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal information internationally, we rely on our providers' standard safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

06How long we keep it

  • Membership and setup details: while you're a member, and for up to 6 years afterwards for tax, accounting and dispute purposes.
  • Affiliate records and payout history: for as long as you're an affiliate, and up to 6 years afterwards.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • The referral code in your browser: 60 days.

07Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it's wrong;
  • ask us to delete it (we may keep what the law requires us to);
  • object to or restrict how we use it, including for marketing;
  • receive a copy in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent you've given.

Email support@thetradevault.co and we'll respond within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority (for example the ICO in the UK, or UODO in Poland), though we'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

09Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information, and passwords are stored only as secure hashes. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us straight away if you think something's wrong.

10Children

The Trade Vault is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, let members know.
