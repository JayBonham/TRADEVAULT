What is a pip in trading?

A pip is the unit traders use to measure how far a price has moved. Once you understand pips and points, you can measure your stop, your target and your risk in the same language as everyone else.

By The Trade VaultUpdated 17 Nov 2026
Key takeaways
  • In most forex pairs a pip is the 4th decimal place (0.0001). In JPY pairs it’s the 2nd (0.01).
  • A pipette is a tenth of a pip, the extra decimal most brokers show.
  • Gold and indices like US30 are usually measured in points, not pips.
  • Pip value depends on your position size: about $10 per pip for a standard lot of a USD-quoted pair.

What is a pip?

Pip is short for “percentage in point” or “price interest point”. It’s the standard unit of movement in a currency pair. For most pairs, like EUR/USD or GBP/USD, one pip is the fourth decimal place: 0.0001.

If EUR/USD moves from 1.0850 to 1.0870, it has moved 20 pips.

Pairs that include the Japanese yen are quoted with fewer decimals, so for USD/JPY one pip is the second decimal place: 0.01. A move from 150.20 to 150.50 is 30 pips.

Pips vs pipettes

Most brokers now quote one extra decimal: EUR/USD at 1.08503 instead of 1.0850. That last digit is a pipette, a tenth of a pip. MetaTrader often calls pipettes “points”, which is why a 20-pip stop can show as 200 points in your platform. It’s the same distance.

Points on gold and indices

Gold, US30, NAS100 and other CFDs and futures are usually measured in points rather than pips:

EUR/USDPips (0.0001)1.0850 → 1.0870 = 20 pips
USD/JPYPips (0.01)150.20 → 150.50 = 30 pips
Gold (XAU/USD)Dollars or points2,400.00 → 2,404.00 = $4 move
US30Points39,000 → 39,040 = 40 points

Traders sometimes call a $0.10 move in gold a “pip”, so always check what someone means before comparing numbers.

How much is a pip worth?

A pip on its own is just a distance. What it’s worth in money depends on your position size:

  • Standard lot (100,000 units): about $10 per pip on pairs quoted in US dollars, like EUR/USD.
  • Mini lot (10,000 units): about $1 per pip.
  • Micro lot (1,000 units): about $0.10 per pip.

For other pairs, the pip value changes with the exchange rate. For gold and indices, it depends on your broker’s contract size, which you’ll find in the symbol specification in MetaTrader.

Once you know the value per pip, sizing is simple. Our position size calculator does the maths for you.

Why pips matter

Thinking in pips and points lets you separate the distance of a trade from the money. Your setup decides the distance to your stop. Your risk rules decide the money. Your position size connects the two. That’s the foundation of every risk-to-reward decision you’ll make.

Frequently asked questions

What is a pip in forex?

A pip is the standard unit of price movement in a currency pair: 0.0001 for most pairs and 0.01 for pairs that include the Japanese yen.

How much is 1 pip worth?

It depends on your position size. For a standard lot of a pair quoted in US dollars, one pip is worth about $10. A mini lot is about $1 and a micro lot about $0.10.

Is a point the same as a pip?

Not always. In MetaTrader a “point” is usually a pipette, a tenth of a pip. On gold and indices, traders measure moves in points or dollars rather than pips.

How many pips is a $1 move in gold?

Conventions differ. Many traders treat $0.10 as a pip, which makes a $1 move 10 pips, but it’s clearer to talk about gold in dollars or points.
