Trading sessions: Asian, London and New York

The forex and CFD markets run nearly 24 hours a day, but they don’t behave the same all day. Knowing which session you’re trading in tells you how much movement to expect.

By The Trade VaultUpdated 17 Nov 2026
Key takeaways
  • Three main sessions: Asian (Tokyo), London and New York.
  • London opens at 8:00 AM UK time (3:00 AM ET). New York’s stock market opens at 9:30 AM ET.
  • The London–New York overlap is usually the busiest part of the day.
  • Match your market to its session: US30 and NQ come alive at the New York open.

The three main sessions

As one financial centre closes, another opens. Roughly:

SessionUK time (winter)Eastern TimeCharacter
Asian (Tokyo)12:00 AM – 9:00 AM7:00 PM – 4:00 AMUsually quieter, ranges often form
London8:00 AM – 5:00 PM3:00 AM – 12:00 PMVolume jumps, big moves in EUR, GBP and gold
New York1:00 PM – 10:00 PM8:00 AM – 5:00 PMUS data and the stock market open drive indices

Exact times shift when the clocks change. The US and UK change on different dates in March and November, so for a couple of weeks the gap is four hours instead of five.

The London–New York overlap

From about 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM ET (1:00 PM to 5:00 PM UK), both London and New York are open. Liquidity is at its highest and many of the day’s biggest moves happen here, especially around US data at 8:30 AM ET and the stock market open at 9:30 AM ET.

Which session for which market?

  • US30 and NQ: the US stock indices are busiest from the New York open at 9:30 AM ET.
  • Gold: active in London and New York, often with strong moves at the London open and around US data.
  • EUR/USD and GBP/USD: most active in London and the overlap.
  • JPY and AUD pairs: see more movement in the Asian session than other pairs.

Using session ranges

Many strategies mark the high and low of one session and trade the reaction at the next. The Asian range is a classic example: price often takes out one side of it in London. Opening range strategies do the same with the first part of a session, which is the idea behind Andrew Bridges’ Spectrum ORB sessions.

Pick a session that fits your life

You don’t need to trade every session. Pick one that suits your market and your schedule, learn how it behaves, and be consistent. One focused hour beats twelve distracted ones, and it’s much easier to keep honest in your trading journal.

Frequently asked questions

What are the main forex trading sessions?

The Asian (Tokyo), London and New York sessions. Sydney is sometimes listed separately at the start of the Asian session.

What time does the London session open?

8:00 AM UK time, which is 3:00 AM Eastern Time for most of the year.

When is the best time to trade?

Volume is usually highest during the London–New York overlap, roughly 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM ET. The best time for you depends on your market and when you can focus.

What time does the New York session start?

Forex activity picks up from about 8:00 AM ET, and the US stock market opens at 9:30 AM ET.
