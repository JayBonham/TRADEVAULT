How it works

Three steps. Every setup.

01 · AUCTION

Initial market auction

Spectrum reads how the session opens, the initial auction, to frame the range the market is working in.

02 · VOLUME

Session volume analysis

It weighs where volume traded in the session to find the levels that matter.

03 · INDUCEMENT

Liquidity inducement

Entries come when the market induces traders the wrong way, then shows its hand.

Questions

Spectrum FAQ.

What is the Spectrum scanner?

Spectrum is an index scanner built by Andrew Bridges. It uses initial market auction theory, session volume analysis and liquidity inducement to find entries on US30, ES and NQ.

What markets does Spectrum cover?

Spectrum is built for the index markets: US30 (Dow Jones), ES (S&P 500 futures) and NQ (Nasdaq). The bootcamp also includes a set file for XAU/USD.

Who trades Spectrum live?

Andrew Bridges runs Spectrum ORB sessions live in The Trade Vault around the London afternoon, four days a week.
