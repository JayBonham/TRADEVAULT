Smartshot FAQ.

What is the Smartshot scanner?

Smartshot is a Smart Money Concepts scanner for gold and NQ, built by Tyler Collins. It maps liquidity and structure on higher timeframes and signals entries on lower timeframes with order-flow confirmation.

What does A+ and B+ mean?

Smartshot grades its setups and only signals the higher-quality ones, A+ and B+. It’s designed to give you fewer, clearer trades rather than constant alerts.
