How it works

Three steps. Every setup.

01 · TREND

Trade with the trend

Killshot reads EMA structure across four timeframes (M3, M15, H1, H4) so you know whether you should only be buying or only be selling.

02 · LOCATION

Wait for the Point of Control

It marks the Point of Control, the price where the most volume traded. Entries near it have a reason to hold. Entries in the middle of nowhere don’t.

03 · TRIGGER

No star, no trade

A signal star prints when price is stretched to an extreme in the direction of the trend. That’s the trigger. Your stop and target are set before you click.

Questions

Killshot FAQ.

What is the Killshot scanner?

Killshot is a rules-based scanner built by Jay Bonham. It combines EMA trend structure, Point of Control levels and signal stars to flag setups on US30, gold and Bitcoin, with the stop and target defined before entry.

Does Killshot place trades for me?

No. Killshot finds and flags setups that match its rules. You decide whether to take the trade, and you place it. The Trade Vault teaches you to read every signal in the Killshot Bootcamp.

Which markets does Killshot work on?

Killshot is used on US30 (the Dow Jones), gold (XAU/USD) and Bitcoin. Jay and Adam trade it live on US30 and gold.

How do I get Killshot?

Killshot is included in every Trade Vault plan: Tools ($49.99/month, all three scanners), Community (pick one educator and one scanner) and Elite (everything).

Can I backtest Killshot?

Yes. The last lesson of the Killshot Bootcamp, “How to use and backtest Killshot”, walks through testing it on past charts before you trade it.
