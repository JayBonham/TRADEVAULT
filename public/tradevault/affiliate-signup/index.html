Affiliate terms

  1. Joining is by application. Your link starts working once you're approved; we'll email you.
  2. You earn 20% of every payment made by a member who joins through your link, including renewals, for as long as their membership stays active. From your 25th paying member, new payments earn 30%. We may change rates for future payments with notice.
  3. Payouts unlock once 5 members you referred have paid. Commission you earn before then is kept and becomes payable when you reach 5.
  4. A member counts as yours if they click your link and join within 60 days, completing their setup on the same device. The most recent affiliate link they clicked wins.
  5. Commission is held for 30 days after each payment to cover refunds and chargebacks. Refunded or charged-back payments earn no commission and don't count towards your totals.
  6. Payouts are sent by PayPal or crypto (USDT or USDC) to the details in your dashboard. Check them carefully: crypto sent to a wrong address or network can't be recovered.
  7. Top affiliates may also receive a quarterly profit share at our discretion.
  8. You can't earn commission on your own membership, and you can't bid on "The Trade Vault" or educator names in paid search ads.
  9. Promote honestly. No promises of profits or guaranteed results, no spam, and no impersonating The Trade Vault or its educators. Trading carries risk; say so.
  10. We may pause an account or withhold commission for fraud or a breach of these terms. Questions: hello@thetradevault.co.
